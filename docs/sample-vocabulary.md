# Vocabulary

## English

| Word | Meaning | Dutch | Greek |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **outcome** | the way a thing turns out; a consequence | uitslag, resultaat | έκβαση, κατάληξη |
| **wary** | feeling or showing caution about possible dangers or problems | omzichtig, behoedzaam, voorzichtig | προσεχτικός, επιφυλακτικός δύσπιστος |
| **thorough** | complete with regard to every detail; not superficial or partial | volmaakt, volledig, volkomen | λεπτομερής, εξονυχιστικός, πλήρης |
