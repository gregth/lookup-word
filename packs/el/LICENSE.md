# Third-party data

## lemmas.tsv.xz

Greek inflected forms and their lemmas (form, tab, lemma; 225,503 rows), exported on 2026-11-01 from
[simplemma](https://github.com/adbar/simplemma) 2.0.0 by Adrien Barbaresi (software: MIT License). Lookup Word reads
it to find the dictionary headword of a Greek word (είδε → βλέπω).

simplemma builds its lists from these sources, each under its own licence:

- [Lemmatization lists](https://github.com/michmech/lemmatization-lists) by Michal Měchura: Open Database License
- Wiktionary entries packaged by the [Kaikki project](https://kaikki.org/): CC BY-SA
- [FreeLing](https://github.com/TALP-UPC/FreeLing), [spaCy lookups data](https://github.com/explosion/spacy-lookups-data),
  the [UniMorph project](https://unimorph.github.io/), and the
  [Wikinflection corpus](https://github.com/lenakmeth/Wikinflection-Corpus) by Eleni Metheniti (CC BY 4.0)

The derived list stays under those terms: keep this notice with it, and share changes to it alike.
